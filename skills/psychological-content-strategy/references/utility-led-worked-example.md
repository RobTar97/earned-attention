# Worked example: Check a curtain swatch at your window

[Utility-led scripting guide](utility-led-scripting.md) · [English script](#english-script) · [日本語版](#日本語版)

Kitehem Studio（カイトヘム）is a fictional made-to-order curtain studio. This is a production plan for a short on the studio’s own account. The footage and offer below are stipulated for the example.

## Brief and available proof

**Viewer and goal.** Someone choosing curtains wants to see how a fabric looks in the room where it will hang. Teach a check they can repeat with any sample. Give interested viewers one next step once they’ve seen the check.

**Project voice.** Like a helpful person in the shop. Use short observations and familiar words. In English, name the thing the viewer can see. In Japanese, use warm, conversational です・ます. Example voice cues for this brief are “Have a look at the weave by the window” and 「窓にかざして、織り目を見てみましょう」.

**Offer.** The studio’s profile links to a live fabric swatch request form in the viewer’s language. The action is to request a sample. The form is separate from ordering curtains.

| Clip | What the editor has |
|---|---|
| A · Window wide | The presenter holds one swatch at the window, where a curtain would hang. |
| B · Weave close-up | The same swatch held still against the window. A hand slides an off-white backing card away and daylight makes the weave easier to see on this sample. |
| C · View from the sofa | The same swatch at the same window, seen from where the viewer normally sits. |

The clip labels refer to described material. All times below are positions in the proposed edit. The demonstration supports what this fabric looks like in this room’s daylight.

## Three hook options

Every option leads into the same check: hold the swatch where it would hang, then look from your usual seat. Select one opening before building the cut.

| Direction | English opening | 日本語のひと言 | First frame → first useful beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Show the change** | “Same fabric. Look at the weave in the light.” | 「同じ生地です。光を通すと、織り目が見えてきます。」 | Start on B with the card already moving. Hold on the weave, then cut to A to show how to set up the check. |
| **2. Catch a missed check** | “Before you pick a curtain fabric, check it at the window.” | 「カーテンの生地を選ぶ前に、窓にかざしてみてください。」 | Open on A with the swatch at the window. Move into the weave detail in B so the viewer knows what to look for. |
| **3. Start where the viewer sits** | “How will this fabric look from the sofa?” | 「ソファから見ると、この生地はどんなふうに見えるでしょう？」 | Open on C from the seat. Cut to A to show where the swatch is held, then return to the sofa angle for the answer. |

**Select 1 for both versions.** Clip B shows the weave becoming easier to see, and the short observation lets the footage do the explaining. The cut to A turns the detail into a check viewers can set up at home.

## English script

**Title:** How to check a curtain fabric swatch in daylight

| Edit time | Spoken line | Picture and cut | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | “Same fabric. Look at the weave in the light.” | Open tight on B as the card slides away. Hold the exposed weave for a beat. | Same fabric, window light |
| 4–9s | “Hold the swatch up where the curtain would hang.” | Cut to A. Keep the position of the swatch against the window clear. | Hold it at your window |
| 9–15s | “Step back to where you usually sit. See how the weave looks from there.” | Cut to C. Through the whole line, hold the view from the sofa. | Look from your usual seat |
| 15–19s | “Check each swatch from that same spot.” | Continue on C with the swatch and the surrounding light in frame. Give the instruction time to read. | Same spot, same light |
| 19–26s | “Want to try it at your window? Request a swatch through our profile.” | Return to the clear fabric detail in B. Hold the closing text until the end. | Request a fabric swatch • Link in profile |

**Post caption:** Choosing curtain fabric? Hold a swatch where the curtain will hang and look at it from the seat you use most. Compare other swatches from the same spot, in the same light. To try it at home, request a fabric swatch through our profile.

## 日本語版

**タイトル**　カーテンの生地サンプル、窓辺でどう見る？

| 編集時間 | ナレーション | 映像と編集 | 画面テキスト |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4秒 | 「同じ生地です。光を通すと、織り目が見えてきます。」 | Bの寄りから。台紙をずらして、織り目が見えるところで一拍置く。 | 同じ生地を、窓の光で |

| 9–15秒 | 「いつも座る場所から、織り目がどんなふうに見えるか確かめます。」 | Cに切り替え、ソファからの見え方をそのまま見せる。 | いつもの席から見てみる |

**投稿キャプション**　カーテンの生地を選ぶときは、取り付ける窓にかざして、いつも座る場所から見てみてください。別の生地と比べるときも、同じ場所・同じ光で。生地サンプルはプロフィールからお申し込みいただけます。

## Before filming

- Read each version aloud with its edits. The 26-second timings are estimates. Give the weave and the last line enough time to register in each language.
- Keep the overlays short and clear of the weave. Use accurate speech captions and check that the sample position and comparison instruction also read on mute.
- Preview each profile link and match the closing words to the request form. The video has already delivered the window check by the time the invitation appears.
