## How to classify a claim

- **Verified user fact:** use as supplied unless contradictory; do not expand beyond its scope.
- **Current platform guidance:** check the platform's primary documentation and actual placement. Label ad evidence as ad evidence.
- **Research finding:** inspect the original study, conditions, outcome, population, and effect before stating an exact claim. A book model or marketing case study is not a universal law.
- **Production heuristic:** useful starting point to test, clearly labeled. Examples include an optional editorial hook rubric, approximate beat allocations, and reviewing progression every 1–3 seconds.
- **Creative hypothesis:** plausible audience response, to be tested rather than asserted as established fact.

## Corrections and qualifications applied

| Source assertion or tension | Synthesis decision |
|---|---|
| System 1 continuously overrides System 2; intuitive persuasion bypasses scrutiny | Retain fast/slow processing as a model; support both relevance and informed consideration |
| Colors, typography position, or familiar faces have universal effects | Make them contextual composition and audience hypotheses |
| Visual illustrations increase recall by exactly 65% | Omit the number; exact effect requires checking the original study |
| A 30–60-second hook/introduction is standard | Identify long-form context; short-form openings earn relevance immediately |
| Open many questions before giving value | Use one main promise and limited supporting gaps with early answers |
| 60:40 is immutable; brand building requires a universal three-year minimum | Treat allocation and horizon as context dependent; prescribe neither automatically |
| Acquisition/loyalty success percentages apply to every business | Omit universal numerical comparisons and blanket targeting rules |
| A cut or reveal produces a dopamine hit | Describe observable creative intent; no neurochemical measurement is implied |
| A fixed safe zone or words-per-second rule fits all video | Verify placement and readability; do not generalize ad figures universally |
| More cuts, more views, or replay spikes mean better results | Inspect meaning, satisfaction, and business quality; spikes can signal confusion |

These qualifications preserve the practical ideas while removing false certainty. Most book and neuroscience claims in the supplied material were not independently verified; use this reference as an evidence boundary, not a scientific literature review.
