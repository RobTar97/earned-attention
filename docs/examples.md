# Example prompts

Replace the fictional products and constraints with your real information. Explicitly naming the skill is useful when your agent has several overlapping marketing skills.

## 1. Create a product-demo Reel

```text
Use psychological-content-strategy to write a 30-second Instagram
Reel for LoopList. It saves and reuses project checklists; it does
not automate tasks. Audience: small agency team leads who repeat
similar projects. We have one screen recording and no testimonials
or measured time savings. Use warm, practical English. Give exactly
three hook angles, choose one, and deliver a timed beat table with
speech, proof, headline text, and sound/edit notes. End with one
free-trial CTA and suggest one experiment.
```

Expect a concrete opening, an honest demonstration, continuous timing, a complete payoff, and a test with a quality/business guardrail. Unseen footage is treated as a production plan, not inspected evidence.

## 2. Repair a weak educational Short

```text
Use psychological-content-strategy to improve this 45-second Short.
Audience: project managers. The first four seconds show a logo.
The next eight explain why clear checklist items matter. The examples
do not appear until second 26. The ending asks viewers to like,
share, follow, and book a call. The useful lesson is to replace
"Review design" with "Check the design on mobile", "Check copy"
with "Confirm the headline names the customer outcome", and
"Test form" with "Submit it and confirm the success message".
Rank the problems using this evidence and give the complete rewrite.
Keep one CTA. Do not claim to have watched footage.
```

Expect evidence-linked priorities and an immediately useful opening. The rewritten asset should still deliver all three checks.

## 3. Interpret a retention chart

```text
Use psychological-content-strategy to diagnose this 30-second video.
Retention: 3s 70%, 8s 45%, 20s 25%. A screenshot at 18s has a replay
spike. We have 10,000 starts and 40 shares; reach is unavailable.
The screenshot contains six dense lines. Separate observations from
possible causes, explain what cannot be concluded, and propose one
controlled-variable test with a primary metric and guardrail.
```

Expect hypotheses rather than a claimed causal diagnosis. A replay spike can signal value or difficulty reading; rates should use available, labeled denominators.

## 4. Write concise headlines and catchcopy

```text
Use psychological-content-strategy to give three English headlines
and three Japanese catchcopy options for team leads. Product truth:
LoopList saves and reuses project checklists. We have no automation,
testimonials, or measured savings. Keep the voice calm and concrete.
Deliver only the copy and one sentence explaining the angles.
```

Expect copy-sized output. The skill should follow the requested scope instead of adding a video storyboard or full campaign plan.

## 5. Plan a small brand-and-trial campaign

```text
Use psychological-content-strategy to build a six-asset organic
content plan for LoopList. We can record one screen demo and two
talking-head clips each week. Brand cues: a lime circle and
"Start from your best process". Connect useful advice to project
kickoff and wrap-up moments. Balance recognition with free-trial
acquisition, give each asset a primary measure, and explain how
we should choose the mix without assuming a fixed 60:40 ratio.
```

Expect a feasible mix of original recordings and derivatives, distinct brand-memory and acquisition measures, and a contextual allocation decision.

## 6. Adapt one concept across platforms

```text
Use psychological-content-strategy to adapt this completed tutorial
for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Keep the same
demonstrated claim and complete payoff. Suggest changes to the first
frame, title/cover, caption, and CTA. Verify any exact current
platform requirement before using it. We will edit in our existing tool.
```

Expect platform-specific packaging decisions grounded in the same creative truth. Rendering or publishing requires your own authorized tool workflow.
